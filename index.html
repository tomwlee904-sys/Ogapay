FAQ

Common questions

How do I get paid? +

Once a task poster approves your submission, funds are released instantly. You can withdraw to your Nigerian bank account via Paystack or to your Solana wallet as USDC.

Do I need crypto experience to earn? +

Not at all. You can earn and withdraw entirely in Naira. Crypto is optional — but easy to set up with a free Phantom wallet if you want USDC payouts.

What is the minimum withdrawal? +

₦500 for Naira withdrawals and $1.00 USDC for crypto withdrawals. There are no fees for Naira withdrawals under ₦10,000.

How do I post a task as an advertiser? +

Create an account, fund your wallet (NGN or USDC), then click "Post a Task". Set your reward, number of slots, proof requirements, and go live in minutes.
