Common questions

How do I get paid?

Once a task poster approves your submission, funds are released instantly. Withdraw to your Nigerian bank account via Paystack or to your Solana wallet as USDC.

Do I need crypto experience to earn?

No. You can earn and withdraw entirely in Naira. Crypto is optional.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

₦500 for Naira withdrawals and $1.00 USDC for crypto withdrawals.

How do I post a task as an advertiser?

Create an account, fund your wallet, then post a task with reward, slots, and proof requirements.
