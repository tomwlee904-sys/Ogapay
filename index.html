FAQ

Common questions

How much can I earn per day?

Earnings depend on the time you invest and the types of tasks you complete. Simple social tasks pay less, while freelance tasks (like design or writing) pay much more. Active users typically earn between ₦2,000 to ₦10,000+ daily.

Do I need a crypto wallet to use OgaPay?

No! You can operate entirely in Naira using bank transfers and Paystack. Crypto (USDC on Solana) is completely optional but recommended for those who want instant international settlement.

How do I get approved for tasks?

Once you submit proof for a task, the advertiser reviews it. If you followed all instructions, they will approve it and the funds will be released to your balance. Many tasks feature auto-approval after 24 hours to protect workers from inactive advertisers.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal is ₦1,000 for local banks and 1 USDC for crypto wallets. Processing is automated and usually completes within minutes.
