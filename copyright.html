Copyright Policy

OgaPay respects the intellectual property rights of others. Users are required to ensure that all content they submit, including task descriptions, proofs, and products, does not infringe on the copyright or trademark rights of any third party.

Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used on OgaPay without authorization, please send a notice to support@ogapay.ng with the following: your contact information, description of the copyrighted work, the URL of the infringing content, and a statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized.

Consequences

Users who repeatedly infringe copyright may have their accounts suspended or terminated. All disputes are reviewed within 5 business days.
